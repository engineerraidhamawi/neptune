NEPTUNE SERPENT

The First Autonomous Arab Naval Combat Drone

Overview

Neptune Serpent is a multi-mission autonomous underwater combat drone. It operates both underwater and on the surface, with unmatched stealth, maneuverability, and cost efficiency — at less than 10% the cost of conventional torpedoes.

Key Specifications

Length4.2 meters (MUTANT model)
Diameter0.8 meters
Weight370 kg
Warhead150–250 kg
Max Speed45 knots (Underwater) / 65 knots (Surface)
Range650 km
Operating Depth400 meters
